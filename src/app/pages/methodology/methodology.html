Sources & methodology

How we can be certain

The methods and standards that keep every story in this archive honest.

01

Truth first, always

Every story represents a real person, organisation or event. We never invent conversations, achievements, statistics or moments. If a real account is less dramatic than a fictional one, we publish the real one.

02

Source-based reporting

Wherever possible, each account is grounded in verifiable references:

  • Reputable newspapers & reporting
  • Books and published biographies
  • Academic publications
  • Government and institutional websites
  • Official organisations
  • Documented interviews & documentaries
  • Historical archives
03

Verification status

Every story carries a verification badge. When sources are independently confirmed, it reads “Sources verified.” For well-documented historical figures we mark them “Historical sources.”

04

Honouring uncertainty

We never hide doubt. Where facts are uncertain we say so plainly; where sources contradict, we present the uncertainty rather than choosing the version that sounds better. A caveat is not a failure — it is fidelity.

05

Real imagery only

Photographs come from Wikimedia Commons, public-domain and government archives, and licensed sources. We never pass AI-generated images off as photographs, and we credit authors and licences wherever required.
