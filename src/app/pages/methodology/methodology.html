Sources & Methodology

How we verify the stories we tell.

Truth First

Every story on this platform represents a real person, organization, or event. We never fabricate stories, invent conversations, or manufacture inspirational moments.

Source-Based

Each story references reliable sources wherever possible, including:

  • Reputable newspapers
  • Books
  • Academic publications
  • Government websites
  • Official organizations
  • Interviews and documentaries
  • Historical archives

Verification Status

Each story displays a verification status. When sources have been independently verified, it shows "Sources Verified." For historical figures with standard archival documentation, it shows "Historical Sources."

Handling Uncertainty

The platform must never sacrifice truth for a better story. If facts are uncertain, we say they are uncertain. If sources contradict each other, we show the uncertainty rather than choosing whichever version sounds better.

Images

Images come from Wikimedia Commons, public-domain archives, official organizations, and licensed photography. We do not use AI-generated photographs presented as real. Attribution is shown where required.
